## Refunds and failed PayPal payments

Evoxt’s pricing page and FAQ do not state a refund window, a refund method, or whether a refund goes back to PayPal or arrives as account credit. That is the biggest gap for a PayPal buyer, and it matters more because Evoxt sells prepaid terms up to three years. Ask support in writing before you pay: what is the refund window for a first order, and how is the refund issued? If the answer is account credit, the refund only helps if you plan to stay.

PayPal’s dispute process is a fallback, not a guaranteed refund. Eligibility depends on PayPal’s rules for your transaction and country. Use it after you have a support ticket and a clear record of what was promised.

If PayPal declines the payment, the cause is usually inside the PayPal account: the funding source, a regional restriction, or an account limit. Try another card or your PayPal balance first. Evoxt also accepts cards directly, Bitcoin, and USDt Tron, so a PayPal decline does not have to end the order. If PayPal shows the payment as complete but the Evoxt invoice still shows unpaid, send support the PayPal transaction ID.

## Which Evoxt plan fits your workload

The plan name matters less than RAM, storage, and transfer. A static site, a DNS node, or a lightweight tunnel endpoint can run on VM-0.5 at $2.99, but 512 MB RAM and 5 GB storage are tight for a control panel or a database. A small WordPress site with a cache plugin fits VM-1 at $5.99, where 2 GB RAM and 20 GB storage leave room for the OS and a few plugins.

An application plus MySQL or PostgreSQL on the same box needs VM-2 at $11.99, with 2 cores and 4 GB RAM. A Docker host with several services also starts at VM-2 if RAM is the limit; choose VM-3 at $14.99 when you need 4 cores and 4 GB RAM. Multiple sites or a busier database fit VM-4 at $23.99, with 4 cores and 8 GB RAM. For compile jobs and CPU-heavy builds, VM-3 or VM-4 is the sensible range, since the higher-core plans cost more than the workload usually justifies.

Evoxt’s pricing page does not list Windows templates. If you need Windows, ask support before ordering. The same applies to a specific Linux distribution or a custom ISO.
